Mama · Maker · Creator

I'm the creative heart behind Bliss Press Boutique, where I bring thoughtful ideas to life through personalized designs made especially for you.

I'm a proud mom living my Florida dream just a few miles from the Most Magical Place on Earth—and I love bringing a little of that joy and magic into everything I create.

From custom party kits and cake toppers to personalized gifts, every creation is designed with care and close attention to the little details. I believe those details are what make a celebration feel truly personal—and turn an ordinary item into something worth remembering.

My style is clean, modern, and timeless, with a playful touch when the occasion calls for it. I love working closely with each customer to create something that complements their theme, reflects their vision, and feels uniquely theirs.

Bliss Press Boutique is about more than simply creating products. It's about helping you celebrate life's sweetest moments with something beautiful, meaningful, and made just for you.

Thank you for supporting my small business and allowing me to be a small part of your special memories. I can't wait to create something beautiful with you!
